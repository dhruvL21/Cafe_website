# **App Name**: Cup o' Joy: Immersive Cafe Experience

## Core Features:

- Interactive Menu: A visually engaging menu with animated elements showcasing each item, allowing users to explore ingredients and preparation methods.
- Storytelling Animation: Animated shorts about the cafe's history, coffee sourcing, or community impact, creating an emotional connection with visitors.
- Virtual Cafe Tour: An immersive, animated 3D tour of the cafe space, showcasing its unique design and ambiance.
- AI-Powered Coffee Recommendation: A tool which analyzes user preferences (e.g., flavor profiles, mood) to suggest personalized coffee and pastry pairings.
- Animated Location: The map uses custom marker animations when displaying the location(s) of the cafe(s).

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Warm beige (#F5E7D0), evoking the comforting feel of coffee and baked goods.
- Background color: Light beige (#F9F3ED), provides a soft, inviting backdrop.
- Accent color: Burnt orange (#D98E57), representing the rich, roasted hues of coffee beans, for interactive elements.
- Headline font: 'Belleza', a serif to reflect fashion, art and design.
- Body font: 'Alegreya', a serif used for longer body texts.
- Custom-designed icons with subtle animations, representing coffee types, ingredients, and cafe amenities.
- Full-screen, parallax scrolling layout with asymmetrical content arrangement to create a sense of discovery and uniqueness.
- Micro-interactions on hover states, page transitions, and content reveals to provide playful, delightful feedback.
